Hi {{ name }}, your {{ org_name }} workspace is ready. These simple habits help protect your team's information and keep your CRM data dependable.

Protect access

  • Use a unique, strong password and enable multi-factor authentication wherever available.
  • Invite only the teammates who need access and give each person the least-privileged role they need.
  • Remove access promptly when a teammate leaves or changes responsibilities.
  • Review active sessions, connected applications, API tokens, and your security audit log regularly.

Keep data dependable

  • Keep customer and company records accurate; merge duplicates instead of creating copies.
  • Review imports, exports, and integrations before sharing sensitive information.
  • Back up important business data and periodically check that your team can access what it needs.

Godan CRM provides role-based access and an organization audit log to help administrators review important activity. Check your available controls in the workspace.
